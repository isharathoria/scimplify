02  /  What's working

The front end is further along than the "lost vision" story

Real, structured demand

RFQs carry CAS, purity, volume, required equipment and certifications. Example: PROJ-1102, HFPO, 100 MT/yr, NLT 98%, pressure-rated reactors. Directories list products; Scinode lists projects.

A three-sided loop

Buyer RFQ → matched plants (ATOMS) and labs (IONS) → proposals → Scimplify executes. The inside-sales workflow, partly productised.

Monetisation already being tested

Priority Partner at ₹5 lakh/yr: unlimited proposals, exclusive projects, intel reports, demand generation for 5 products.

The right schema, waiting for data

Reaction types, reactor capacity in KL, materials of construction, EHS and utilities: exactly what unit-operation matching needs.

03  /  What's missing

The gap is underneath: data a buyer, a plant and an AI can trust

01

Network numbers disagree

Research partners: 1,000+, 600+, 400+ and 200+ on different pages. ATOMS intelligence counters show "0+".

02

Taxonomy is drifting

"Dyes & Pigments" and "Dyes and Pigments", four coatings variants. Any typed value joins the master list.

03

Demand data is noisy

~60 RFQs posted on 25 Oct share one volume (0.5–1 MT/yr). Bitumen sits under Agrochemicals at 34 g.

04

Capability is self-declared

"Can do nitration" is not "ran nitration at 5 KL, 92% yield, 2026". Nothing links profiles to executed batches.

05

No reason for plants to return daily

ATOMS is all about leads. Nothing helps run the plant, which is where Harsh's batch-record idea fits.

06

Trust is a banner, not a mechanism

"SCINODE SECURE" is on the dashboard, but there are no public data-use or IP terms. Four brands blur one system.
